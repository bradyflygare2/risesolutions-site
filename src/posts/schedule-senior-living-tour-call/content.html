What Happens During the Call

When someone calls to book a tour, showing or appointment, how you handle that call decides whether it becomes a visit. A simple process helps you get more of those calls on the calendar.

  • Answer the Call Promptly: Picking up quickly shows you're on it, and the caller is more likely to stay on the line.

  • Transfer the Call if Possible: If the relevant staff member is available, transferring the caller right away makes booking the appointment smoother and faster.

  • Voice Receptionist's Role: When the right person cannot answer, the voice receptionist’s job is to take a clear, detailed message and notify appropriate staff immediately.

  • Text the Tour Schedule: During the call, send the tour or showing time by text so the caller leaves with the details on their phone.

Each step is there so booking the visit is easy, and so fewer leads disappear after the first call.

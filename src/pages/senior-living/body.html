Questions

What administrators ask us

Does it replace our receptionist?

No. It covers nights, weekends, lunch breaks and overflow, so your receptionist can focus on the people in the lobby.

Can families still reach a person?

Yes. Callers are transferred to your staff whenever they ask for a person or the call needs one.

Do we need new phones or a new number?

No. Rise AI works with your existing phone system and your building keeps its main line.

How long does setup take?

Most buildings are live in days, not weeks.

Is Rise HIPAA and SOC 2 compliant?

Yes. Rise Reception and Rise Messaging are built on HIPAA and SOC 2 compliant platforms. We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every healthcare and senior living client before go-live.
